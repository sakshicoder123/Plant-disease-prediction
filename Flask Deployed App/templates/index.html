General Plant Health Rules
  1. Sanitation: Disinfect pruning shears between plants to stop pathogen transmission.
  2. Watering: Water at the base/soil level rather than over foliage to discourage fungal spore germination.
  3. Crop Rotation: Rotate Solanaceae (tomatoes, potatoes, peppers) annually to starve soilborne diseases.
  4. Air Circulation: Prune dense branches to allow breeze and quick leaf drying.
